01Strategy Case Study

Growing Access to A Free Mentorship Program (Indonesia Mengglobal, 2021-2024)

As Social Media Manager for this nonprofit, Munissa owned the full strategy loop behind the organization’s flagship mentorship program, from defining who the program wasn't yet reaching, to reporting what moved the numbers.

Audience

Indonesian students and young professionals who are actively seeking scholarship and study-abroad opportunities, often priced out of paid consultants and wary of misinformation about "who gets to apply."

Objective

Grow qualified applications to the free mentorship program by heavily relying on organic interactions and limited budgets for paid media and online advertising.

Strategy

Built a recurring editorial plan that published testimonials from past mentees and mentors, along with Q&A sessions with the internal team of Indonesia Mengglobal to provide deeper and more detailed explanations about the program.

KPI Tracked

Mentee and mentor application volume.

Result

+35% YoY mentee applications and +35% mentor applications.
